About

Aiden Reyes
Creative Director / Visual Artist / Designer

Based between Tokyo and Lisbon, I explore how visual systems communicate what language cannot. My practice spans brand identity, editorial design, generative art, and spatial installations — unified by a belief that great design is always in conversation with its context.

Previously at Pentagram and IDEO. Currently collaborating with cultural institutions, independent studios, and technology companies that care about the texture of their presence in the world.
